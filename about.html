Our Approach

A modern, hands-on consulting service

One More Thing is a modern, hands-on consulting service that helps you organize essential information and create clear access plans for the people you trust most. We work with individuals and families across Plano, Frisco, Allen, and Richardson, Texas.

Most people have the information their loved ones would need in an emergency — it's just scattered across accounts, notebooks, and memory. OMT brings it all into one guided session, and turns it into a plan that's organized, accessible, and entirely under your control.

Whether it's a weekend away, a medical procedure, or simply planning ahead, our goal is the same: give you confidence, and give the people you trust the clarity they need to support you without confusion or stress.
