Can a Family Member Notarize a Document for You in Texas?

Short answer: generally, no. Here's why impartiality matters, and what it means for families managing paperwork for a loved one.

If you're a caregiver trying to get a power of attorney, medical directive, or other document signed for a parent or spouse, you might wonder if you — or another family member — can simply notarize it yourselves. In Texas, the answer is generally no. A notary is required to act as a neutral, disinterested witness, and that impartiality breaks down when the notary has a personal, financial, or family relationship to the document being signed.

Why Impartiality Is the Whole Point of Notarization

A notarization exists to confirm that the person signing a document is who they say they are, is signing willingly, and understands what they're signing. That confirmation only means something if the notary has no stake in the outcome. When the notary is related to the signer, or benefits from the document in any way, that neutral check disappears — even if everyone involved has good intentions.

What This Means for Family Caregivers

Caregiving families often deal with a stack of important paperwork: powers of attorney, healthcare directives, guardianship documents, and financial authorizations. It's common for an adult child or spouse to be the one helping gather and organize these documents. That's completely appropriate — but the actual notarization should come from someone outside the family, with no personal interest in the document's contents.

This isn't about mistrust. It's a safeguard that protects everyone, including the person signing, by keeping the process above question later on.

Why an Independent Mobile Notary Helps

Bringing an independent notary to the home takes the guesswork out of who should sign off on important paperwork. It also removes the burden of transporting an aging parent or homebound family member to an office. A mobile notary comes to you — whether that's a house, a care facility, or a hospital room — so the signer can complete everything in a familiar, comfortable setting.
