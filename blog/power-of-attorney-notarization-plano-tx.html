Power of Attorney Notarization in Plano, TX

2026-10-24

A power of attorney is one of the most common documents people need notarized, and it often comes up during a stressful or time-sensitive moment. Whether you're helping an aging parent, managing a move, or preparing for a medical situation, getting it notarized correctly matters.

What Happens During Notarization

A notary's job is to verify your identity, confirm you're signing willingly, and witness the signature. They are not responsible for explaining what the document means or whether it's the right type of power of attorney for your situation — that's a conversation to have with an attorney beforehand if you're unsure.

Once you know the document is ready to sign, the notarization itself is usually quick. The main things to have ready are a valid photo ID and the unsigned document (most notaries ask that you don't sign until they're watching).

Why People Choose a Mobile Notary for This

Power of attorney documents are sometimes needed by someone who can't easily travel — due to age, illness, or a busy schedule. A mobile notary comes to your home, office, or another agreed location in Plano, which removes the hassle of finding and driving to a notary's office.

This is especially helpful when multiple people need to be present for the signing, or when the person signing feels more comfortable in a familiar place.

What to Check Before You Book

  • Confirm the document is complete and ready to sign, aside from the signature and notarization.
  • Make sure the signer has a valid, unexpired photo ID.
  • Decide on a location and time that works for everyone who needs to be present.
  • Ask how payment works — OMT Services accepts cash, check, and credit card.

Beyond the Notarization

A power of attorney is often just one piece of a larger plan for making sure the right people can access important information when it's needed. If you're already thinking about who should be able to act on your behalf, it may be worth considering what other documents or access details should be organized at the same time.
